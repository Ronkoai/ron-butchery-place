Before you write

Three things worth knowing

i.

Will you travel?

Yes — I have taught and consulted in Hong Kong, Singapore, Laos, Cambodia and the United States in the last two years, and I travel readily when the work calls for it.

ii.

Do you work with pork and lamb too?

Yes. Beef is where my reputation sits, but I learned the trade on pork carcasses, and have run plants processing beef, pork and lamb, including charcuterie and dry-aging programmes.

iii.

Can you work in a factory as well as a kitchen?

That is the unusual part of my CV: HACCP plant set-up, SOPs, yield and cost, ready-to-heat product development, and the digital systems that record all of it.
